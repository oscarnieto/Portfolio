01 About

I turn brand strategy into visual systems people actually remember. Ten years of posters, identities and screens — built on type, grids, and a healthy disrespect for both.

I’m Iris — an independent designer working between Barcelona and wherever the project lives. I partner with founders, studios and cultural institutions to build identities that survive contact with the real world: airports, lockscreens, tote bags, 3 a.m. pitch decks.

Previously design lead at Studio Rauw (AMS) and Otra (BCN). My work has been recognised by ADC, D&AD and the Type Directors Club.
